# iscanna launch plan

## The pitch

**One line:** Scan a recharge card and dial it in one tap.

**The problem:** Loading airtime means typing `*121*` or `*133*`, then 16 digits from a scratched
strip, then `#`. One wrong digit and the recharge fails, and you start again.

**The answer:** Point the camera at the strip. iscanna reads the PIN, checks it, and builds the
code. You tap Dial.

**Why trust it:** It works offline, has no ads and no account, and photos and PINs never leave
the phone. It's an independent tool, not affiliated with NetOne or Econet.

## Who it's for

1. **Everyday prepaid users** who load a card a few times a week. Message: "Stop typing PINs."
2. **People who load several cards at once:** families, small offices, anyone who buys cards in
   bulk. Message: "Load a stack of cards in a minute." (Batch mode.)
3. **Airtime sellers: tuckshops, vendors, kiosks** who load cards for customers all day.
   Message: "Serve customers faster, with fewer failed recharges." These are also the best
   word-of-mouth channel: every customer sees the app in use.
4. **People with poor eyesight, or on cracked screens and worn cards,** for whom the 16 digits
   are the hardest part.

## Channels (in order of expected impact)

1. **WhatsApp:** Status, Channels and groups are where people in Zimbabwe share things.
   - A 15-second screen recording on your Status (script below), with the Play link.
   - Ask 10 to 20 friends to repost it on their Status on launch day.
   - Share in community, campus, church and business groups where it's welcome; don't spam.
2. **TikTok, Reels and Shorts:** the same vertical demo, plus "before vs after" (typing a PIN
   vs scanning it). Real hands, real cards, a real phone.
3. **X (@exxstatix):** a launch thread with the demo video, the "why I built this" story and the
   store links. Pin it.
4. **Facebook groups:** Zimbabwean tech, small business and buy-and-sell groups.
5. **In person:** show three or four nearby airtime sellers and help them install it. Their
   customers will ask what it is.
6. **Tech media and communities:** a short note with the video and screenshots to Zimbabwean
   tech blogs and newsletters (for example Techzim and TechnoMag) and local developer communities.

## 15-second demo video script

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Thumb scratching a card, then a hand typing a long PIN and getting it wrong | "Typing 16 digits?" |
| 3–8 s | iscanna: point camera at the strip, the PIN appears | "Just scan it." |
| 8–12 s | Tap Dial, the dialer opens with the full code, call goes through | "Dial in one tap." |
| 12–15 s | App icon and name, store badge | "iscanna · Scan - Recharge · Free" |

Film vertically in good light, keep the phone steady, and blur or use a used card so no live PIN
is shown.

## Launch week

- **Before launch:** recruit the 12+ Play closed testers (you need them anyway); ask each for an
  honest rating and one sentence of feedback once it's live. Record the demo video. Publish the
  privacy policy. Prepare posts in advance.
- **Day 1:** WhatsApp Status and groups, X thread, TikTok/Reels. Ask friends to share.
- **Days 2–3:** Facebook groups. Visit airtime sellers. Reply to every comment and question.
- **Days 4–7:** Post a second clip showing Batch mode. Send the note to tech media. Gather
  feedback and ship a small update (it shows the app is alive and it lifts store ranking).

## Store search (ASO)

- Words people search for: recharge, airtime, voucher, top up, scan, PIN, USSD, NetOne, Econet,
  Zimbabwe. They're already in the listing copy and App Store keywords.
- Ratings drive ranking: after someone has used the app a few times, a polite in-app review
  prompt helps (a good next feature).
- Screenshots do most of the selling: the first two (Scan, Dial) carry the message.

## What to measure

- Installs per day (Play Console, App Store Connect), and which channel caused the spikes.
- Rating and review text: fix whatever people complain about first.
- Uninstalls in the first week (Play Console): high numbers mean the first scan is too hard.
- Questions in DMs and comments: each one is a sign of an unclear screen or missing feature.

## Ideas for later

- In-app review prompt after a few successful dials.
- "Share iscanna" link in About.
- Shona and Ndebele translations.
- Telecel cards, if their recharge format is confirmed.
- A small landing page with the video, store badges and the privacy policy.

## Things to avoid

- Don't use NetOne or Econet logos or brand colours in ads or graphics; name them in text only,
  as the listing does.
- Never show a real, unused PIN in a video or screenshot.
- Don't buy fake installs or reviews; both stores penalise it.
