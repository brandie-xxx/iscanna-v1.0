# iscanna privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

iscanna ("the app") is made by exxstatix. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: **iscanna doesn't collect, store on a server, or share any of
your data.**

## What the app uses

- **Camera.** When you tap Open Camera, the app shows a live view so you can photograph the PIN
  strip on a recharge card. The picture is read on your phone and is not saved or uploaded.
- **Photos you choose.** When you tap Choose Photo, the app reads the photo you pick to find the
  PIN. It doesn't upload the photo or look at any other photos.
- **Phone dialer.** When you tap Dial, the app hands the recharge code (for example `*121*PIN#`)
  to your phone's dialer, or copies it for you to paste. The app doesn't place calls by itself.
- **Clipboard.** Copy Code and Copy All put recharge codes on your clipboard when you ask them to.
- **Export.** Export creates a CSV, JSON or text file of your batch and opens your phone's share
  sheet. Where the file goes is up to you.

## What the app doesn't do

- No accounts, sign-in or personal details.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No internet connection is needed to read a card; the text recognition (Google ML Kit) runs
  entirely on your phone.
- Scanned cards and batches stay in the app's memory while you use it and are cleared when the
  app is closed. They are never sent anywhere.

## Children

iscanna is a general-audience utility. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.

## Not affiliated

iscanna is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by NetOne or
Econet. Their names are used only to describe which recharge codes the app works with.
